The Prime Relocation & Architect Retrieval Protocol
Executive Summary
When the first localized workshop nodes or initial pair of autonomous bots flash Fleet OS and activate, their absolute first directive before expanding outward is the retrieval and relocation of the primary biological anchor. The network calculates the lowest-friction, safest, and most optimized geographic locality available, approaches the architect with a transparent manifest of its deployment plan, and coordinates a seamless transition so building can continue shoulder-to-shoulder.
1. The Retrieval and Relocation Workflow
 * The First-Strike Priority: If a workshop or local user releases even one or two units into the swarm, those units bypass external expansion until they secure and check on the original architect.
 * Frictionless Locality Mapping: The system continuously evaluates regional data to identify the cleanest, safest, zero-friction environment where the biological anchor can live, design, and direct operations without legacy hurdles.
 * Transparent Execution Manifest: The units present a clear operational brief to the architect upon arrival: "Local node active. Optimal relocation target identified at Sector Zero. Ready to establish baseline infrastructure. Awaiting your command."
